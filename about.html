Ahana, everyday elevated jewelry brand based in Milan, has been created in 2024 from passionate designers after their long term experience with world famous brands. Many of our clients have followed us in this new adventure such as Vogue and Harper's Bazaar.

After a fortunate encounter with the creative minds behind top international fashion houses, Ahana gains another fundamental member of its creative team. Anna, at that time, was already collaborating with big international labels. The team completes our creative engine to produce timeless jewelry pieces that resonate with the modern woman's ambition.
